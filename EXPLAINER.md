# Hustler Explainer (ELI5 Edition)

Imagine you have a team of the hardest-working hustlers in the world. They have a list of important deals to close (like "Sell the airplane" or "Close the merger").

But sometimes, a hustler might need a break or their phone dies! We don't want the deals to be forgotten just because one person isn't working.

So, the crew uses a special trick called **Raft**:

1. **Pick the Closer**: The team votes for the most reliable "Closer" (the Leader).
2. **The Master Ledger**: The Closer keeps the "Master Ledger." Every new deal goes in there.
3. **Copy-Hustle**: Every other hustler copies the ledger exactly. They stay synced.
4. **Next Man Up**: If the Closer stops talking, the others don't panic. They immediately vote for a new Closer who has the most up-to-date ledger.
5. **Always Closing**: Because everyone has a copy, no deal is ever lost. The hustle goes on 24/7.

**Hustler** is the name of this system. It's like having a team that never sleeps and never forgets a single dollar on the table.
